What We Do

We’re a student chip-design club at UW–Madison. Learn digital logic, write and test hardware code, and work toward a chip design with other students. You can join without prior experience.

Our first project is an open-source LLM inference accelerator: an open-weight language model running on an FPGA, supported by a custom instruction set, compiler, simulator, and Python runtime.

We also plan to invite engineers from companies such as NVIDIA, Apple, AMD, and Intel to talk about their work and answer members’ questions.

From AI to Silicon

AI Model

Defines the neural network and its computations.

Compiler

Translates the model into hardware-targeted instructions.

ISA

Defines the instructions the processor can execute.

RTL

Describes the digital logic that implements the processor.

CHIP

The physical silicon that executes the computations.
